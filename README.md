ST10509429 Musa Maluleke
## Project overview
History: STE Autobody was founded to provide reliable, high-quality car repair and bodywork services to the local community. Since opening, the business has built a reputation for skilled panel beating, paint restoration, and mechanical repairs, growing through word-of-mouth referrals and repeat customers
## Website Goals and Objectives
Generate leads from customers needing repair quotes.
Build trust through visible reviews, before-and-after photos, and certifications.
Make it easy to request a quote or book a repair online.
Reduce phone-only enquiries by offering a contact and quote form.
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): Number of quote requests submitted, contact form conversions, average time on site, growth in organic search traffic, and click-throughs to phone or directions
### Proposed Website Features and Functionality
Homepage with services overview and a clear call-to-action ("Get a Free Quote").
About Us page detailing team, experience, and certifications.
Services page (dent repair, paint jobs, collision repair, insurance work, etc.).
Gallery of before-and-after repair photos.
Contact page with quote request form, phone number, map, and opening hours.
