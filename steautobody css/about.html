About STE Autobody

STE Autobody was founded to provide reliable, high-quality car repair and bodywork services to the local community. Since opening, we have built a reputation for skilled panel beating, paint restoration, and mechanical repairs, growing through word-of-mouth referrals and repeat customers.

Our Mission

To deliver honest, expert car repair services that get customers safely back on the road, with quality workmanship and fair pricing.

Our Vision

To become the area's most trusted name in auto body repair, known for craftsmanship, reliability, and customer care.

Our Values

  • Honesty - clear quotes with no hidden costs
  • Craftsmanship - repairs finished to a high standard
  • Speed - realistic timelines communicated clearly
  • Care - we handle the paperwork so you don't have to
